Welcome! This site explores the story and sound of an iconic polka instrument passed through the Tomiak family.

Here you'll find a digital instrument allowing composition with the Chemnitzer's unique sound, an archive
of manuscripts my great-grandfather owned, and a series of articles describing the instrument's history
and function.
